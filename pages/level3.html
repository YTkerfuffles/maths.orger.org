Level 3 – A level maths

Levels 2, 3 and 5 are meant to simulate what you would learn from GCSE and A level textbooks. The contents are meant for you to read to get an idea of what you need to know, or to skim if you think you already know it just to fill in gaps and pitfalls, but you should study it more formally alongside this if you go through the documents and don't feel you have done it in school or basically know it. Proofs for some things in here are deferred to levels 4 (for pure) and 6 (for stats).
