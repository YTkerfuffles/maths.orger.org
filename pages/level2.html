Level 2 – GCSE level maths

Levels 2, 3 and 5 are meant to simulate what you would learn from GCSE and A level textbooks. The contents are meant for you to read to get an idea of what you need to know, or to skim if you think you already know it just to fill in gaps and pitfalls, but you should study it more formally alongside this.

Latest expected release date for Level 2: January 2nd 2026.
